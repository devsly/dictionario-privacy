# Dictionario Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 15, 2026**

2S Pixels ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Dictionario mobile application ("Dictionario" or the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Dictionario.

## 1. Information We Collect

Dictionario may process the following information depending on the features you use:

### Account information

If you create or use an account, Dictionario may process information associated with your account, such as your email address and authentication information.

Authentication and account-related services may be provided using Google Firebase services.

### Dictionary and learning data

Dictionario may store information that you create or use within the App, including:

- dictionary words and their details;
- examples, categories, tags and favourites;
- learning progress;
- review and learning statistics;
- recent words and learning-session information.

Some of this information is stored locally on your device using the App's local database.

If synchronization is enabled, relevant application data may also be synchronized using our cloud services.

### Purchase information

Dictionario may offer optional Premium functionality through a one-time in-app purchase.

The Premium product is identified as:

`dictionario_premium`

Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your complete payment card number or other complete payment credentials.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information processed by Dictionario is used to:

- provide and operate the App;
- authenticate users;
- synchronize your application data when synchronization is enabled;
- save and restore your dictionary and learning progress;
- provide learning and review functionality;
- provide Premium functionality;
- maintain the security and reliability of the App;
- respond to support requests.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 3. Local Storage

Dictionario is designed with local storage as an important part of its architecture.

Dictionary and learning information may be stored directly on your device. Information stored locally may remain on your device even when you are not signed in.

Deleting the App may remove locally stored application data, depending on your device and operating system.

## 4. Cloud Services and Synchronization

When you use account-based synchronization, information necessary to provide synchronization may be processed by cloud service providers used by Dictionario.

Dictionario uses Google Firebase services for account authentication and synchronization functionality.

The information synchronized may include application data associated with your account, such as dictionary and learning data.

## 5. Google Play

Dictionario is distributed through Google Play and may use Google Play Billing for Premium purchases.

Google may independently process information according to Google's own privacy policies and terms.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 6. Data Sharing

We may use service providers that process information on our behalf when necessary to operate Dictionario.

These may include:

- Google Firebase, for authentication and cloud services;
- Google Play, for application distribution and in-app purchases.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or property of 2S Pixels, our users, or others.

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by Dictionario.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally on your device can generally be removed by deleting the relevant data or uninstalling the App.

For account-associated information stored through cloud services, you may contact us to request deletion of your account-associated information.

Please send deletion requests to:

**dictionario.app@gmail.com**

We may need to verify the request before processing it.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or where reasonably necessary for legitimate legal, security, or accounting purposes.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Dictionario is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at:

**dictionario.app@gmail.com**

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Dictionario, our services, or applicable legal requirements.

When we make changes, we will update the effective date shown at the top of this page.

We recommend checking this page periodically for the latest version.

## 11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, privacy practices, or your personal information, please contact:

**2S Pixels**

Email: **dictionario.app@gmail.com**
