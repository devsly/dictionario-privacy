Dictionario – Account Deletion

This page provides instructions for requesting deletion of your Dictionario account and associated data.

How to delete your account

If you can access the Dictionario app, you can delete your account directly from the account settings inside the app.

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request account deletion by contacting us using the email address below.

To request account deletion:

Send an email to:
dictionario.app@gmail.com

Please use the email address associated with your Dictionario account and include the following subject:

Dictionario – Account Deletion Request

Request Account Deletion

What data will be deleted?

When an account deletion request is processed, the Dictionario account and associated user data will be deleted in accordance with the Dictionario Privacy Policy.

This includes account information and dictionary and learning data associated with the account.

Data retention

Some information may be retained where required or permitted by applicable law, security requirements, fraud prevention, or other legitimate legal obligations.

Any applicable retention will be handled in accordance with the Dictionario Privacy Policy.
